# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary visitors**

- Creators (streamers, short-form, gaming-adjacent) evaluating whether Shaky is a credible long-term partner for capital, systems, and operator support.
- Brands, platforms, and investors who need a grounded read on Shaky as an operator: selective, technical, not a volume agency.

**Context**

They arrive from a link, search, or intro. They skim on desktop or phone, often at night or between sessions. They are deciding trust in under a minute: "Are these real operators, or another pitch deck?"

**Job to be done**

Understand what Shaky does today, who it is for, and how to reach out without hype or vague promises.

## Product Purpose

Shaky Gaming & Entertainment is a Toronto-based creator support studio (since 2020). The site is the public face of that work: explain the thesis (creators as small media companies), what support looks like now (capital, systems, operator time), and how to contact the team.

Success looks like: the right creators and partners self-select in; wrong-fit visitors understand the boundaries (no guarantee factory, not platform-locked, not a huge roster).

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** Technical · Craft · Quiet confidence

**Voice**

- Direct and specific. Short sentences. No buzzwords.
- Operator-first: loadouts and systems, not lectures.
- Gaming-native energy without neon crypto or esports scream.

**Emotional goal**

Grounded trust: "These people build and invest. They will be clear about scope and timing."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero metric blocks, three identical icon cards, purple gradients).
- Creator agency pitch decks (promise language, roster flex, hype).
- Cyber HUD clichés (corner brackets, scanlines, fake status pings, stacked glass cards).
- AI editorial pivot (cream paper, magazine serif essay layout as a substitute for identity).
- Neon crypto / Web3 aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Evolve the dark mood, don't erase it.** Night-mode, brand blue, and atmosphere are part of Shaky; the goal is craft, not a category swap to "safe light editorial."
2. **One signature motion, not a stack of effects.** A single memorable treatment (e.g. geometry) done well beats vignette + grid + scanlines + grain + glass together.
3. **Show operator credibility, not agency theater.** Stats and facts belong in context, not as a SaaS hero-metrics template.
4. **Game-studio atmosphere, studio restraint.** Reference esports and game-studio sites for energy and color; avoid HUD cosplay and template chrome.
5. **Words earn their place.** Copy stays aligned with the site: no restated headings, no intro that repeats the title.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pragmatic baseline: readable type sizes, sufficient contrast on dark surfaces, visible focus states, and full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` (static fallback when motion is decorative). No formal WCAG audit target yet; avoid motion-only information.
